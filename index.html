Shorts Publisher

A private publishing tool for one creator's own channels.

Shorts Publisher is a personal tool that uploads short vertical videos, made by its owner, to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. It runs only on the owner's computer and talks directly to each platform's official API.

Before anything is published, the owner reviews and approves each video. The tool never posts to anyone else's account, and it is not offered as a service to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video with its title and description to the owner's connected accounts.
  • Reads basic account information (such as the channel name) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Checks whether an upload succeeded.
